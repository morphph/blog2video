# Visual Style Prompt

## Video Generation Prompt

Generate a technical education video in the style of a solo-creator "whiteboard explainer." The video uses a **screen-recorded digital whiteboard (Excalidraw)** as the primary visual, with a **webcam picture-in-picture (PiP)** of the presenter in the bottom-right corner (~15-20% of frame width). There are no slide decks, no B-roll footage, and no stock imagery — the entire visual narrative is delivered through a single large hand-drawn diagram that the camera pans and zooms into as the presenter walks through different sections.

### Composition and Layout

- **Main canvas (80% of frame):** A full-screen capture of the Excalidraw whiteboard application. The Excalidraw toolbar is visible on the left edge, giving an authentic "working in real-time" feel. The browser chrome (tabs, URL bar) is visible at the very top, reinforcing the screen-recording aesthetic.
- **Webcam overlay (bottom-right):** A rectangular PiP window showing the presenter from the chest up. The webcam has a slightly blurred home-office background with natural/warm lighting on the face. The presenter is a young man wearing glasses and casual attire (polo or t-shirt), who occasionally gestures while speaking.
- **Captions (bottom-center):** YouTube-style auto-generated captions with a semi-transparent dark background and white text, displayed in two lines. The captions have a subtle yellow/highlighted tint.

### Diagram and Visual Style

The core visual is a **single large hand-drawn system architecture diagram** in Excalidraw's signature style:

- **Hand-drawn aesthetic:** All boxes, arrows, and borders use Excalidraw's sketchy/hand-drawn line style, giving a casual whiteboard feel rather than polished corporate graphics.
- **Color coding system:**
  - **Red/orange handwritten text** for section titles (e.g., "Harness", "Loop")
  - **Orange dashed-line borders** for major grouping areas
  - **Pink/coral filled ovals** for central concepts (e.g., the LLM Agent node)
  - **Green filled rectangles** for input/output nodes (e.g., "User Prompt", "Reply")
  - **White/light gray rectangles** with black text for standard process boxes
  - **Database cylinder icons** in gray for storage elements
  - **Black arrows** showing data flow direction between components
- **Typography:** A mix of Excalidraw's hand-drawn font for labels and clean sans-serif for longer descriptions. Section sub-text uses a smaller font in parentheses (e.g., "(e.g. LangGraph, LangChain, Pydantic)").
- **Layout:** The diagram flows left-to-right and top-to-bottom, with the main agent run pipeline in the center, memory systems below, and an operations/evaluation section to the right.

### Key Text Callout

During the introduction, a **large text block** appears on the right side of the diagram listing the 4 key concepts in bold, stacked typography:

```
Harness
Loop
LLMOps
Eval
```

This serves as a visual anchor for the viewer to understand what the video will cover.

### Transitions and Camera Movement

- **No hard cuts or slide transitions.** The entire video takes place on one continuous whiteboard canvas.
- **Smooth zooming:** The presenter zooms in and out of different sections of the diagram as they explain each concept. For example, zooming into the "Harness" section, then panning to the "Loop" area, then zooming out to show the full system.
- **Progressive reveal:** New sections of the diagram come into view as the camera pans, creating a sense of exploration and building complexity.

### Audio and Pacing

- **Solo narrator:** One male presenter speaking in a conversational, educational tone. Casual but knowledgeable — "let me walk you through this."
- **Pacing:** Moderate speed, ~150 words per minute. Each concept gets 2-4 minutes of explanation.
- **No background music** during the main content. Clean audio from a desk microphone.
- **Chapter markers** visible in the YouTube progress bar, dividing the video into named sections.

### Thumbnail Style

The thumbnail features:
- The presenter's face/upper body on the right side
- The whiteboard diagram visible in the background/left side
- Bold text overlay with the video topic
- A branding watermark ("SEAN'S STORIES" top-left, "YOU CAN BUILD ANYTHING" top-right)
- Clean, high-contrast design optimized for YouTube click-through

### Overall Aesthetic

Educational tech explainer with a **casual, approachable, indie-creator vibe**. Think "smart friend explaining a complex topic on a whiteboard" rather than a corporate training video. The visible Excalidraw toolbar and browser chrome create authenticity — this is a real person working through a real diagram, not a pre-rendered animation. The hand-drawn style lowers the intimidation factor for complex technical concepts, making the content feel accessible to both technical and non-technical audiences.
